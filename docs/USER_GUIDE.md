# User guide: Alarm From Hell

Alarm From Hell won't let you turn it off with one tap. When it rings you have to beat a set of
challenges (move, think, talk, or go find something) before it goes quiet. By then you're awake.

## First launch: make sure it can ring

Open **Settings** (gear icon) and check **Will it actually ring?**. Everything marked red needs a tap on **FIX**:

| Item | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Notifications | The alarm appears through a notification. Without it the alarm can't show itself. |
| Full-screen alarm | Lets the alarm take over the lock screen. |
| Exact alarms | Rings at 6:30, not "around 6:40". |
| Microphone | Needed only for the Word on Beat challenge. |
| Camera | Needed only for the Find the Object challenge. |
| Physical activity | Optional. More accurate step counting for the Steps challenge. |
| Unrestricted battery | Optional extra safety against battery savers. |

Then tap **Ring a test alarm in 10 seconds** and lock your phone. If the alarm takes over the lock
screen at full volume, you're set. The home screen also shows a red warning card whenever something
required is missing.

## Creating an alarm

Tap **+** on the home screen.

- **Time**: scroll the wheels. The line underneath shows how long until it rings.
- **Repeat**: tap days, or use *Once*, *Weekdays*, *Weekends*, *Daily*. A one-time alarm switches
  itself off after it rings.
- **Label**: optional, shown on the ringing screen.
- **Difficulty**:
  - **Easy**: one simple challenge
  - **Normal**: two random challenges
  - **Hard**: three different challenges, tougher settings
  - **Nightmare**: five randomized challenges with strict checks. Mistakes can reset your progress.
- **Challenges**: pick the pool the alarm draws from. If a challenge needs the camera or microphone,
  the app asks for permission when you select it.
- **Challenges to beat**: how many you must complete (1–8). Choosing a difficulty sets a default.
- **Try these challenges**: runs them right now, with no alarm and nothing recorded.
- **Sound**: six built-in sounds (Hellfire Siren, Nuclear Klaxon, Panic Beeper, Stadium Air Horn,
  Demon Bell, Gentle Sunrise) with preview, or any ringtone on your phone.
- **Volume**: how loud, as a share of your phone's maximum alarm volume.
- **Extreme mode**: while ringing, the alarm volume is locked and the volume buttons do nothing.
- **Gradual volume**: starts quiet and reaches full volume after about 45 seconds.
- **Vibrate**
- **Snooze**: on or off, length (3/5/10/15 min) and limit (1/2/3/5).
- **Wake-up check**: see below.

Tap **Save alarm**. On the home screen, toggle alarms on and off, tap one to edit it, or long-press to delete.

## When it rings

The ringing screen shows the time, a taunt, and the challenges between you and silence.

- **I'm up. Prove it →** starts the challenges.
- **Hold to snooze** (if allowed) has to be held for two seconds. **Every snooze adds one challenge**
  to the next round.
- Back does nothing. If you leave for another app, the alarm screen comes back within about 20
  seconds and the notification re-appears if you swipe it away.
- Restarting the phone doesn't help: an interrupted alarm comes straight back after a reboot.

During challenges:
- The progress bar at the top shows how many are left.
- Lie still for too long and you're sent back to the ringing screen ("Your alarm isn't going anywhere").
  The current challenge restarts; completed ones stay completed.
- If a challenge can't run on your phone right now (permission turned off, no speech engine), it's
  swapped for another one. Word on Beat, Dance and Find the Object also offer a swap after you've
  struggled for a while.

When you finish: confetti, **"Congratulations, you're finally awake!"**, how long it took, your fails
and your streak.

## The challenges

| Challenge | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Word on Beat** | The alarm goes quiet and a beat starts. All the words are shown up front; say each one out loud when it lights up. Accents and sleepy mumbles are fine (it listens for how the word *sounds*); harder levels just want it clearer. Nightmare uses two-word phrases. |
| **Find the Object** | After a quick shuffle you're told what to find. With the default **Afghan home** items that's things like a piyala or tea glass, the chai thermos, naan, the deg, the dastarkhan, the toshak, the pardah, chaplak or shoes at the door, fruit, the sink, toilet or fridge (switch to *General* in Settings). Put it inside the square and hold steady while the square fills green. Harder levels pick things further from your bed and reject pictures shown on a screen. There's a flashlight button and a few rerolls if you don't have the object. |
| **Typing** | Type the phrase exactly before time runs out; a counter shows how many letters you have right. Pasting is blocked. Hard and Nightmare are case- and punctuation-sensitive; Nightmare can be random characters. |
| **Math** | Solve problems on the keypad. In Nightmare a wrong answer resets you to zero. |
| **Shake** | Shake the phone hard until the counter is full. In Nightmare, stopping for three seconds makes progress melt away. |
| **Steps** | Walk. Steps are counted by the phone's step sensor. |
| **Jumps** | Jump with the phone in your hand (hold on tight). |
| **Dance** | Follow the moves on the beat: tilt left, tilt right, palm up (phone flat), shake it, spin. Each move must be struck while it's on screen. |

## Wake-up check

If enabled, a few minutes after you beat the alarm (3 by default, see Settings) a notification asks
**"Are you awake?"**. Tap it or **I'M AWAKE** within 100 seconds. If you don't, the alarm rings again,
challenges and all.

## Adaptive difficulty

When you crush a challenge (fast, no mistakes) it gets a bit harder next time: more shakes, more
steps, faster beats, less time to type. When you struggle it eases off. Levels range from 0.8× to 1.6×
and are shown in Stats. You can switch this off in Settings.

## Stats

The bar-chart icon on the home screen shows:
- day streak, average time to get up, total wake-ups, and "times called lazy" (failed attempts)
- a 7-day chart of time from first ring to finished (tap a day for details, or switch to the table)
- per-challenge wins, fails, average time and adaptive level
- wake-up check results

A missed alarm (one that hits the **Give up after** limit in Settings) breaks your streak.

## Settings

- **Give up after**: 15 min / 30 min / 1 hour / never. After that the alarm stops and counts as missed.
- **Wake-up check after**: 2 / 3 / 5 / 10 minutes.
- **Find the Object items**: *Afghan home* (default) or *General*.
- **Adaptive difficulty**: on or off.
